# Reports Store

`reports/` is a generated artifact store.

## Canonical report tiers

### Shareable

- `reports/shareable/CODEFINDER_Discord_Summary.pdf`
- Audience: informal research sharing
- Scope: German/Kempten only
- Claim level: cautious summary, not final attribution

### Research archive

- `reports/final_report/summary.txt`
- `reports/final_report/final_report.html`
- `reports/final_report/CODEFINDER_Forensic_Report.pdf`
- Supporting generated artifacts such as proof images, sort reports, and statistical reports

### Internal review artifacts

- `reports/manual_review/` for pair-level German/Kempten review sheets and ledgers
- `reports/negative_control/` for repo-local control runs and comparison memos

## Rules

- Prefer regenerating artifacts from scripts over editing them by hand.
- Treat bulk images, HTML, and PDF outputs as local/generated by default.
- Keep durable interpretation, contracts, and manuscript prose in `docs/`, not here.
- Older report folders may remain for audit history, but they should not be treated as canonical without an explicit document saying so.
- Shakespeare legacy outputs are superseded by `docs/SHAKESPEARE_INTERNAL_SUMMARY.md` unless a future cleanup regenerates them under a controlled workflow.
